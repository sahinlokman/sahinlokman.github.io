Privacy policy

This policy explains how Clean Up and its service providers handle information. For privacy questions, contact lokman782782@gmail.com.

Gameplay on your device

Clean Up stores level progress, ad-based unlocks and game preferences locally on your device. You do not need a Clean Up account. Removing the app may remove this local progress.

Purchases

Apple processes payments. Clean Up uses RevenueCat to validate and restore purchases and determine Premium access. RevenueCat may process product and transaction information, subscription status, an automatically generated app user identifier, and technical data needed to operate that service. We do not receive your payment-card details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

Advertising and privacy choices

The free version uses Google AdMob for interstitial ads between levels and optional rewarded ads. Google and its partners may process IP addresses, approximate location derived from IP addresses, device or app identifiers, ad interactions, usage and diagnostic information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and operate their services. Clean Up requests advertising consent through Google's User Messaging Platform where required. Advertising privacy choices are available in the app's Settings when required, and tracking permission can be managed in iOS Settings. Premium removes in-game ads. See Google's privacy policy.

Support and this website

If you contact support, we use your email, message and any details you choose to include to answer and investigate your request. This page is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process connection logs to deliver and secure the site. This page has no advertising or analytics scripts. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Retention and your choices

Local game data remains on the device until removed there. Apple, Google, RevenueCat and GitHub retain data they process under their own policies and applicable law, and may process it outside your country. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information we can identify and control, or to exercise applicable privacy rights. Deleting the app does not cancel an Apple subscription or erase Apple's transaction records. Manage subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.

Children and changes

Clean Up does not offer chat or public uploads. Parents and guardians should supervise purchases and review device privacy settings. If you believe a child sent personal information through support, contact us. We may update this policy when the app or its services change; the update date appears above.

Subscription information

Clean Up is licensed under Apple's Standard End User License Agreement.

Clean Up Premium is an optional one-month, auto-renewable subscription. While active, it removes in-game advertising and unlocks all chapters. The price shown in the app and Apple's purchase confirmation is the price that applies in your storefront. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm. The subscription renews unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends; Apple charges for renewal within the 24 hours before that period ends. Manage or cancel it in your Apple Account subscription settings. Cancellation stops future renewals, while access normally continues until the paid period ends.

Use “Restore purchases” with the same Apple Account to recover an eligible active subscription. Internet access is needed for purchase validation. Removing the app does not cancel a subscription. For billing or refund requests, use Apple's Report a Problem, subject to Apple's rules and applicable consumer rights.

Support

Email lokman782782@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version, app version and the steps that reproduce a problem. For purchase access, connect to the internet and select “Restore purchases” using the Apple Account that purchased Premium. An optional rewarded unlock is only granted when the ad finishes and the reward is reported. Please avoid sending passwords, full receipts or payment-card details.
