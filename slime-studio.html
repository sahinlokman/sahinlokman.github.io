Privacy, terms & support in English

Privacy

Slime Studio does not require an account. Your selected look, audio and haptic settings, slime recipes, local progress and rewarded look expiry times are stored on your device. Removing the app may remove this local data. Apple handles payments. RevenueCat processes purchase history, product and transaction details, subscription status, an automatically generated app user ID and technical data needed to validate and restore Pro access. We do not receive payment card details.

Ads appear only if you choose to watch a rewarded ad. Google AdMob and its partners may process IP address and approximate location, device or app identifiers, ad interactions, usage and diagnostics to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and operate their services. Google User Messaging Platform manages consent where required. You can use the basic app without granting iOS tracking permission. Where applicable, ad privacy choices are available in Settings. See the Google, RevenueCat and Apple privacy policies.

Support emails are used to investigate and answer requests. This page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process IP addresses and access logs; it contains no extra advertising or analytics scripts. Providers retain data under their own policies and may process it outside your country. Contact lokman782782@gmail.com for access, correction or deletion requests where applicable. Local data remains until removed from the device. Deleting the app does not cancel an Apple subscription. Manage tracking permission in iOS Settings. We may update this policy and its date when the service changes.

Terms and subscriptions

Apple's Standard EULA applies. Four looks are free. Completing an optional rewarded ad unlocks the selected extra look on that device for 24 hours. Slime Studio Pro is a monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription that unlocks Pro looks while active. The localized price appears in the app and Apple's confirmation before purchase. Apple charges your account. Unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends, the subscription renews; the renewal charge may occur during that final 24 hours. Canceling stops future renewal and access normally continues to the end of the paid term. Restore purchases in the app with the same Apple Account. Manage subscriptions in Apple Account settings; for billing or refunds, use Report a Problem. Applicable consumer rights are unaffected.
