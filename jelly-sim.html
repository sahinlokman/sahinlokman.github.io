Privacy policy

This policy describes how Jelly Sim and its service providers process information. Contact the developer at lokman782782@gmail.com for privacy questions.

Game data on your device

Jelly Sim stores game progress, scores, discovered characters, cosmetic selections, Jelly Tokens, tutorial state, daily rewards and sound/accessibility preferences on your device. Pending Game Center submissions are also stored locally. No separate Jelly Sim account is required. Local progress is not a cross-device cloud-save service; removing the app can remove that progress.

Purchases and RevenueCat

Apple handles payment. Jelly Sim uses RevenueCat to validate purchases, restore subscriptions and determine Premium access. RevenueCat processes purchase history, product and transaction information, subscription status, an automatically generated app user identifier and technical information needed to provide this service. Jelly Sim does not receive your payment-card details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

Advertising and privacy choices

The free version supports Google AdMob rewarded and interstitial ads. When advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may process IP addresses (including approximate location derived from an IP address), device or app identifiers, ad interactions, usage and diagnostic information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and maintain their services. Ad requests are gated by Google's User Messaging Platform consent state. Where required, a consent form appears and advertising privacy choices are available in the game's Settings. Device tracking permissions can also be managed in iOS Settings. Premium disables in-game advertisements. See Google's privacy policy and Google advertising information.

Game Center

If you use Game Center and it is available, scores and unlocked achievements are sent to Apple using your Game Center player identity. Your Game Center name and results may be visible to other players according to Apple's settings. Manage Game Center through your device settings. Local gameplay remains available without Game Center.

Support and this website

If you contact support, your email address, message and any details you choose to send are used to respond and investigate your request. Do not send passwords, card details or unnecessary personal information. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process IP addresses and request logs to deliver and secure the site. This page adds no advertising or analytics scripts. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Retention, sharing and your choices

Local data remains until it is removed from the device. Apple, Google, RevenueCat and GitHub retain the information they process under their respective policies and applicable legal requirements, and may process it outside your country. Information is shared as described above to operate purchases, advertising, Game Center and support. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information we can identify and control, or to exercise applicable privacy rights. We may need enough information to locate your records. Deleting the app does not cancel an Apple subscription or erase Apple's transaction records. You can cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.

Children and changes

Jelly Sim has no in-app chat or public user-content upload. Parents and guardians should supervise purchases and review device privacy settings. If you believe a child has provided personal information through support, contact us. We may update this policy when the app or its services change; the update date appears above.

Terms of use & subscriptions

Jelly Sim is licensed under Apple's Standard End User License Agreement (EULA). That agreement governs use of the app. The following explains Jelly Sim's features and subscription operation.

Jelly Premium

The optional Premium product is a one-year, auto-renewable subscription. While active, it removes in-game ads, provides one ad-free continue per eligible Classic round and lets you claim a 60-Jelly-Token chest once per calendar day. Gameplay rules still apply; Daily Challenge and Puzzle runs do not gain a continue. Jelly Tokens are in-game items and have no cash value.

The localized annual price is displayed on the Premium screen and Apple's purchase confirmation before purchase. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm. The subscription renews unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Apple charges for renewal within the 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel renewal in your Apple Account subscription settings. Cancellation stops future renewals; active access normally continues until the paid period ends.

Restore and billing help

Use Restore purchases in Premium with the same Apple Account to recover an eligible active subscription. Internet access is needed for purchase validation. App deletion does not cancel a subscription. Billing and refund requests are handled by Apple at Report a Problem, subject to Apple's rules and applicable consumer rights.

Availability and local progress

Advertising, purchase validation and Game Center depend on service availability and connectivity. Local progress may be lost when removing the app or changing devices. These notes do not limit rights provided by applicable law or Apple's EULA.

Support

Email lokman782782@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version, Jelly Sim version and steps that reproduce the problem.

  • Learning to play: open Settings and choose Replay tutorial.
  • Sound: check device volume, silent mode and the game's sound, music and ambience switches.
  • Subscription: connect to the internet and choose Restore purchases in Premium with the purchasing Apple Account.
  • Ads: a rewarded continue is available only when an ad is ready. An unavailable or unfinished ad gives no reward.
  • Game Center: check your Game Center sign-in in iOS Settings. Local play does not require sign-in.

Please contact support before reinstalling, as local game progress may be lost. Do not send passwords, full receipts or payment-card details.
