Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 03, 2026

Welcome to Dreamlit: Bed Time. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information you may share with us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle data when you use our iOS mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Your privacy is important to us, especially given our focus on children's well-being. Therefore:

  • We do not require you to create an account.
  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or physical address to use the core features of the App.
  • We do not collect or transmit any biometric data.
  • We do not track your precise geographical location.

2. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our App is designed for children and families. We are committed to complying with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect, request, or maintain personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

Any interactive elements, such as external links to support or our privacy policy, are protected behind a Parental Gate to ensure that young children cannot navigate out of the App without adult supervision.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising

To support the continued development of the App, we use third-party services that may collect automated, non-personal analytical or advertising data. Specifically:

  • Google AdMob: We use Google AdMob to display advertisements in the App. AdMob may use device identifiers or anonymous data to serve appropriate ads and measure their performance. For more information on how Google uses data, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

We ensure that all ad units served within our application adhere to strict family and child-directional guidelines provided by ad networks to prevent inappropriate content from being displayed to younger audiences.

4. Local Data Storage

The App may store data locally on your device (e.g., cached audio files or your sleep timer settings) to enhance your user experience, such as allowing you to listen to stories offline. This data remains on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately when they are posted.
